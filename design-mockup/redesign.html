AI FAQ

Common questions.

Do we need lots of data to get started?

Usually not. Many useful tools, like customer assistants and document capture, work with the documents, FAQs and records you already have. We'll tell you honestly in the strategy session.

Will AI replace my staff?

Our focus is taking repetitive work off your team's plate, such as answering the same questions and retyping invoices, so people can spend time on customers and higher-value work.

Is our customer data safe?

Yes. We design with POPIA in mind, keep data access to a minimum and can host solutions in environments that meet your security requirements.

Can it work with WhatsApp, email or our existing systems?

That's the point. We connect AI into the channels and software you already use, so there's no new system for your team to learn.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. We start with a focused pilot so you can see value before committing to anything bigger. Book a strategy session for an estimate.
